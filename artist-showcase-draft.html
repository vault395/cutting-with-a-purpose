Artist Onboarding Checklist

  • Artist-approved display name, short biography, and profile photograph
  • 5–10 well-lit artwork photographs, with titles and dimensions
  • Preferred media, subjects, and commission types
  • Availability, pricing policy, turnaround expectations, and shipping/pickup arrangements
  • Approved contact/social links and payment responsibilities
  • Written permission to display images and agreement on credit, ownership, and any reproduction rights

Vault 395 should not reproduce, modify, or laser-engrave the artist's work without separate permission.
