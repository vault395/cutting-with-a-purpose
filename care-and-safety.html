Mirror Care & Safety

Vault 395 creates decorative glass mirrors, including UV-reactive designs. Please review these general precautions and any product-specific instructions supplied with your order.

Glass and handling

Glass can break and cause cuts. Handle carefully, especially at corners and edges. Inspect the item and packaging before handling. Do not use a cracked, chipped, or unstable mirror. Keep broken glass away from children and pets and dispose of it safely.

Display and mounting

Use a stable, appropriate display or mounting system rated for the actual weight of your finished mirror and compatible with the wall or surface. Mounting hardware is not assumed to be included. Do not hang a mirror using adhesive, hooks, or fasteners unless they are rated and suitable for that particular installation. If uncertain, consult a qualified installer. Do not place where a falling mirror could injure someone.

Cleaning and environment

Clean gently with a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive tools and harsh chemicals on artwork, coatings, frame finishes, and backing. Keep decorative mirrors away from excessive heat, moisture, and impact unless a particular product is explicitly rated for those conditions.

UV-reactive artwork and optional lighting

“UV-reactive” describes the artwork’s visual response under suitable illumination. UV lamps, LED strips, power supplies, and electrical lighting are not included with Vault 395 UV-reactive mirrors; customers provide their own lighting. Use lighting only according to its manufacturer’s instructions, electrical ratings, and eye/skin exposure warnings. Do not stare into UV emitters or assume a light is safe for direct exposure. Stop using damaged wiring or components. Keep electrical components dry.

Shipping and delivery

Unpack carefully and inspect for visible damage before installing or displaying. Contact shopvault395@gmail.com with questions or concerns. This page does not limit rights under applicable law.
