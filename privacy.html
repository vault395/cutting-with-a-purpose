Vault 395 Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Vault 395 / Cutting With a Purpose operates this website to display products and receive custom-order inquiries. Contact: shopvault395@gmail.com.

Information you provide

When you request a quote, we receive information you enter, such as your name, email address, mirror choices, delivery preference, ZIP code when applicable, design instructions and notes. Reference images selected for local preview are not automatically uploaded through the order form; you must send them separately by email if you want us to review them.

How we use it

We use order information to answer inquiries, prepare quotes, communicate about design approval, coordinate production and delivery, and maintain business records. Do not submit payment card details or sensitive personal information in the design or notes fields.

Third-party services

Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Direct quote submissions are processed through Formspree. Gallery likes use Supabase. A third-party visitor-count service may process visit information when the counter loads. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser information under their own policies. Email artwork is handled through our email provider. External shipping-calculator links lead to separate websites.

Browser storage and visitor counting

We use browser storage for language selection, liked designs, and to limit repeat visitor-counter increments. These mechanisms are not a reliable count of unique people. You can clear website data in your browser; doing so may reset your local preferences.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell customer inquiry information to advertisers. Information is shared with service providers needed to operate the site and handle inquiries, and where required by law. Records may be retained as needed for order fulfillment, accounting, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Specific provider retention periods may vary and are subject to verification.

Your questions and requests

Email shopvault395@gmail.com to ask about your information or request correction or deletion where applicable. We may need to retain some information for legal or business-record obligations.
